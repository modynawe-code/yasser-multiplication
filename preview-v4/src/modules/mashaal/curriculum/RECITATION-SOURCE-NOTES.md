# Mashaal KG3 recitation source decision

## Selected for bundling

**King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex — Ibrahim Al-Akhdar — Hafs from Asim**

- Human recitation from an official Saudi Quran authority.
- The Complex explicitly permits the listed digital recitations to be used free of charge in computer applications, websites, broadcast channels, and public/private institutional work.
- The child experience is implemented as **listen → repeat → replay**; the Quran itself is never synthesized by TTS.
- A recitation skill is released only after the required local file is present in `recitation-media-data.js`, its metadata passes validation, and its SHA-256 is recorded.

Official pages:
- https://qc-dev.qurancomplex.gov.sa/quran-audios/
- https://qurancomplex.gov.sa/en/sounds-hafs-akhdar/

## Researched but not approved for bundling

### Child-voice Quran channels
Child-voice material such as Dawood Kids is highly approachable for young children, but the pages reviewed did not expose an explicit redistribution license for bundling the audio inside another application. Treat as **reference/listening candidate only**, not a distributable asset.

### Mushaf Muallim recordings
MP3Quran exposes Mushaf Muallim collections for reciters including Muhammad Siddiq Al-Minshawi and Maher Al-Muaiqly. They are strong teaching candidates, but an explicit redistribution license allowing us to package the files inside this PWA was not established during this review. Do not bundle without permission from the applicable rights holder.

### Open-license community recordings
Open-license Quran recitations exist on Wikimedia Commons. They can be useful technically, but the reviewed items did not improve on the selected source across authority, voice provenance, child suitability, and Saudi-project fit. They remain fallback research candidates, not the default source.

## Curriculum scope
The Saudi KG3 structure and SELS indicators support listening/repetition and reciting some short surahs, but the current authoritative sources reviewed for this project do **not** establish a mandatory named-surah list for KG3. Do not label a chosen starter surah as an official KG3 prescription unless a current authoritative curriculum source explicitly specifies it.
